About the Conference

A Gathering of Veterinary Science and Scholarship

The Annual Scientific Conference of the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine brings together researchers, academics, clinicians, and students to share discoveries and exchange expertise.

Through scientific sessions, lectures, and workshops, the conference supports collaboration and advances animal health, public health, and veterinary education.

Our Vision

To be a leading academic platform that elevates veterinary research and inspires scientific excellence.

Our Mission

To connect knowledge, practice, and innovation by providing a trusted space for scientific dialogue.
